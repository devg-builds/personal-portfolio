01 — About

The story so far.

I didn't choose Computer Science because someone told me to. I chose it because I genuinely couldn't stay away from it. It started young — competitive Karate at regional level, Abacus at national level — disciplines that taught me focus and precision. Those same habits quietly followed me into technology.

By 4th grade, computers had me hooked. That curiosity compounded over the years into something real — 111+ Google Cloud skill badges, an independent research paper on satellite orbital mechanics I wrote in Class 10, and two Cyber Security internships before finishing Class 11.

Right now I'm the founding CTO at Finspire — a non-profit building financial literacy tools for underprivileged students across India. I also believe technology without perspective is incomplete, which is why Model United Nations matters to me alongside everything else.

Currently in Class 11 at DPS Faridabad. Preparing for a BS Computer Science abroad in 2028. Still curious. Still building.
