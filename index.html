01 — About

The story so far.

I didn't choose Computer Science because someone told me to. I chose it because I genuinely couldn't stay away from it. Started young — competitive Karate at regional level, Abacus at national level — disciplines that built focus and precision that quietly followed me into technology.

By 4th grade, computers had me hooked. That curiosity compounded into something real — 111+ Google Cloud skill badges, two independent research papers, two Cyber Security internships, and a CTO role — all before finishing Class 11.

Right now I'm the CTO at Finspire — a non-profit building financial literacy tools for underprivileged students across India. I also believe technology without perspective is incomplete — Model United Nations and NCC Navy Wing are how I learn to lead and think globally.

Orbital Perturbation Dynamics in Low Earth Orbit: A Comprehensive Multi-Force Framework for Satellite Lifetime Prediction and Trajectory Analysis
National High School Journal of Science (NHSJS) · Submitted June 2026
A multi-force computational framework extending beyond the two-body problem — incorporating J₂ perturbation, atmospheric drag, solar radiation pressure, and luni-solar gravity. Orbital Mechanics to Study How Small Velocity Changes Shift Satellite Trajectories
Independent Student Researcher · 2024
Formal 6-page research paper simulating a 650 km circular Low Earth Orbit. Analysed anti-normal and radial-inward burns on satellite inclination and eccentricity. Demonstrated Δv of 5 m/s produces measurable orbital trajectory shifts. Written independently in Class 10 with no institutional support.
